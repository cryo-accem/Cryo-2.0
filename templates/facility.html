How to use the facility

Academic and industrial researchers who want to use ACCEM at IISc for high-resolution data collection follow these steps.

  1. 01

    Contact the EM manager

    Write to the EM manager directly at cryoem.iisc@gmail.com or somnath@iisc.ac.in. A nominal charge applies for cryo-EM grid preparation and microscope usage.

    See rates →
  2. 02

    Share sample details & preliminary data

    Provide sample details and preliminary purification or biochemical data to access the microscope. If not provided, no assurance will be given about grid/data quality.

    Book slots →
  3. 03

    Grid freezing

    Cryo-EM apparatus and accessories are expensive, so only expert users (mainly PIs) prepare samples or hand them over to the EM manager.

    Freezing schedule →
  4. 04

    Screening

    For safety and handling considerations, the first and last grid positions of the cassette will be left unoccupied.

    Screening schedule →
  5. 05

    Data collection

    Only trained users are allowed to operate the microscope (Latitude S software).

    Data collection schedule →
  6. 06

    Data hand-off & processing

    Data are transferred to your own hard drive; processing may take 2–3 days. Full data processing support is not available, but assistance is provided if necessary.

    Find your grids →

Good to know

Before you come

  • Sample details and preliminary purification or biochemical data are required.
  • Bring your own hard drive (not provided by the facility) and arrange collection or shipping of the drive yourself.

Facility rules

  • Only trained users are allowed to operate the microscope (Latitude S software).
  • Only expert users (mainly PIs) should prepare samples or hand over to the EM manager.
  • External and internal users cannot request cryo-freezing, cartridge preparation or cassette transfer.
  • The first and last cassette positions are left empty.

Scheduling

  • Industrial samples will receive preferential consideration.

Acknowledging the facility

If you publish work based on data collected at ACCEM, acknowledge the facility personnel and include this funding acknowledgement. It applies even if data collection is done elsewhere after screening at IISc.

Questions? Contact the EM manager at cryoem.iisc@gmail.com or somnath@iisc.ac.in.
