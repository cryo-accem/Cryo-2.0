Rules

How the charges apply

  • Microscope slot: charged per 24-hour slot used for screening or data collection.
  • Grids: charged per facility-provided grid at the rate for its type. Your own grids carry no grid charge.
  • C-clip and base ring: charged for screening and data collection, for each grid that is not already clipped. Not charged if your lab provides the C-clip and base ring.
  • Handling: quoted per 4 grids and charged pro rata for the actual number of grids, once on the freezing booking (glow discharger and Vitrobot) and once on the screening / clipping or data-collection booking.
  • GST: 18% GST is added to every line for academic and industry users. Internal (IISc) users do not pay GST and provide a Debit Head instead.
