Overview

Cryo-EM at the Indian Institute of Science

The Cryo-electron microscopy facility at IISc features a state-of-the-art 200 kV Talos-Arctica electron microscope (FEI/Thermo Scientific) equipped with a Gatan K2 Summit Direct Electron Detector, automatic data acquisition via the Latitude S software package, and a Talos L120C Transmission Electron Microscope. Typically, the Talos L120C is used for negative stain sample visualization before proceeding to cryo-EM data collection using the 200 kV Talos-Arctica microscope.

  • 200 kVTalos Arctica cryo-TEM
  • K2 SummitDirect electron detector
  • Talos L120CNegative stain screening
  • Vitrobot Mark IVGrid vitrification

Sample preparation

The facility is also equipped with essential sample preparation tools such as the FEI Vitrobot™ Mark IV, Pelco easiGLOW & GloQube glow discharge system, and Q150T ES carbon coater.

  • FEI Vitrobot™ Mark IV
  • Pelco easiGLOW
  • GloQube glow discharge
  • Q150T ES carbon coater

What we support

We provide one of the finest cryo-EM facilities in India, supporting negative staining, cryo-EM sample preparation, high-throughput data acquisition, fast data transfer, secure storage, and automatic movie frame alignment. Researchers are supported throughout sample preparation, data collection, and preliminary image analysis.

  • Negative staining
  • Sample preparation
  • High-throughput acquisition
  • Fast data transfer
  • Secure storage
  • Movie frame alignment

Mission

Our goal is to offer open access to the cryo-EM facility for all researchers through a nominal charge or collaborative model. Renowned faculty, postdocs, and EM managers at IISc provide training on protein purification, sample prep, data collection, and basic image processing. Our broader vision is to establish a national-level cryo-EM hub that benefits researchers across India.

Note: Currently, full-scale data processing support is limited to collaborative projects only.

This facility was developed with support from the Department of Biotechnology, Government of India, through the DBT-BUILDER Program (BT/INF/22/SP22844/2017), and the DST-FIST grant (SR/FST/LSII-039/2015) to the Molecular Biophysics Unit, IISc.
