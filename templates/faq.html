Access

Getting started

Who can use the facility?

Academic and industrial researchers who want to use ACCEM at IISc for high-resolution data collection can contact the EM manager. IISc users, external academic users and industry users can all request slots.

How do I get started, and who should I contact?

Write to the EM manager at cryoem.iisc@gmail.com or somnath@iisc.ac.in before submitting a sample. The instructions page walks through each step, and the team page lists the faculty in-charge and EM manager.

What do I need to provide before using the microscope?

Sample details and preliminary purification or biochemical data. If they are not provided, no assurance can be given about grid or data quality.

Can I operate the microscope or prepare grids myself?

Only trained users are allowed to operate the microscope (Latitude S software). Because cryo-EM apparatus and accessories are expensive, only expert users (mainly PIs) prepare samples or hand them over to the EM manager. External and internal users cannot request cryo-freezing, cartridge preparation or cassette transfer.

Booking

Booking slots

How do I book a slot?

Use the slot booking form and choose freezing, screening or data collection. Current bookings are shown on the freezing, screening and data collection schedules.

How many grids and days can I request?

A screening or data collection request can include 1 to 4 grids. Data collection can be booked for 1 to 4 days; microscope time is charged per 24-hour slot.

Freezing is booked by date on weekdays only, and the facility freezes at most {{ freezing_grid_limit }} grids per day across all bookings. The freezing form shows how many grids are still free on each date.

Do industrial samples receive priority?

Yes. Industrial samples receive preferential consideration.

Charges

Charges and grids

How are charges calculated?

Charges depend on your user category (internal, academic or industry) and cover microscope slots, grids, C-clip and base ring, and handling. 18% GST is added for academic and industry users. See the rates page for the full charge sheet. The final amount is as per the issued charge sheet.

How can I check which of my grids are stored at the facility?

Use Find your grids and enter the username you used when booking. It lists your grids, their current storage position and whether each one is available for booking.

Why are the first and last cassette positions left empty?

For safety and handling, the first and last grid positions of the cassette are left unoccupied.

Data

Data and acknowledgement

How will I receive my data, and is processing support available?

Data are transferred to your own hard drive (not provided by the facility), and processing may take 2–3 days. Please arrange collection or shipping of the drive yourself.

Full data processing support is not available, but assistance is provided if necessary.

How should I acknowledge the facility in publications?

Acknowledge the facility personnel and include this funding acknowledgement:

"The data was collected at the ADVANCED CENTER FOR CRYO-ELECTRON MICROSCOPY FACILITY FOR BIOLOGICAL SCIENCES AT IISc, Bengaluru, India funded by Department of Biotechnology (DBT) Infrastructure Grant, Govt of India and DST-FIST."

This applies even if data collection is done elsewhere after screening at IISc.
